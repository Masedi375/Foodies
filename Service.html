Recipes

BREAKFAST RECIPE

Ingredients:

1 cup flour

1 tbsp sugar

1 tsp baking powder

1 egg

1 cup milk

2 tbsp melted butter

Maple syrup & berries to serve

Instructions:

Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.

Whisk egg, milk, and butter. Pour into dry mix and stir.

Heat pan and pour 1/4 cup batter for each pancake.

Cook until bubbles form, flip and cook 2 min more. Serve with syrup.

LUNCH RECIPE

Ingredients:

2 tortillas

1 grilled chicken breast, sliced

1 avocado, sliced

Handful of lettuce

2 tbsp mayo

Salt, pepper & lemon juice

Instructions:

Spread mayo on each tortilla.

Add lettuce, chicken, and avocado slices.

Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon.

Roll up tightly and slice in half. Perfect for lunchboxes!

DINNER RECIPE

Ingredients:

400g pasta

500g beef mince

1 onion, diced

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 can tomato sauce

1 tsp Italian herbs

Grated cheese to serve

Instructions:

Boil pasta according to package. Drain and set aside.

Fry onion and garlic, add mince and cook until brown.

Add tomato sauce and herbs. Simmer for 15 minutes.

Mix with pasta and serve with grated cheese on top.
